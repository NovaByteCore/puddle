# Puddle

A free, private water tracker for Android. Log drinks, keep Puddle the frog's jar full,
earn new critters, and get gentle reminders through the day.

## Install on your phone
1. Open **Releases** on this page (or `/releases/latest`) on your phone while signed in to GitHub.
2. Tap **Puddle.apk** to download it.
3. Open the download and tap **Install**. The first time, Android asks you to allow installs
   from your browser. Turn that on, go back, and tap Install again.

New versions install over the old one, and your drinks and critters stay.

## What's inside
- 17 drink types with hydration values (coffee counts 80%, alcohol subtracts)
- Hydration score out of 100: amount (70), drink quality (15), pacing (15)
- 8 original critters to unlock with streaks and challenges
- History: 7-day chart, monthly calendar, streaks, drink breakdown
- Smart reminders with "+ 8 oz" buttons right on the notification
- All data stays in the app on your phone
